# BTT Ready Pro — Cross-checked handbook study companion

This version has been expanded using the two PDFs supplied in the conversation:
- **Basic Theory of Driving — The Official Handbook** (90 PDF pages; marked last updated 2 January 2026)
- **Final Theory of Driving — The Official Handbook** (118 PDF pages; marked published 2 January 2026)

## Included
- 192 original, paraphrased flashcards across 23 topic groupings
- 80 practice questions with answers and explanations
- Topic filters, shuffle, review ratings and simple spaced repetition
- Timed 50-question mock exam
- Mistake bank and a seven-day revision plan
- Official resource links and offline caching after first load

## Coverage added from the two handbooks

**BTT book:** licence eligibility and classes; PDL and probation concepts; DIPS overview; mandatory/prohibitory/warning/regulatory/information and special-zone signs; tunnel/cycle/directional/EMAS/facility signs; vehicle markings; road markings and direction/merging arrows; traffic lights, on-road signals and police directions; keep-left, lane discipline, overtaking, road hogging, speed limits, give-way rules, roundabouts, expressways, yellow boxes, U-turns, stopping/parking, seat belts, lights, alcohol, mobile devices, vehicle dimensions/height, following distance and three-second rule; pedestrians/cyclists, emergency vehicles, work zones, school/Friendly/Silver Zones, tunnels, autonomous vehicles, electric motorcycles, car parks, lane-use signs, eco-driving, vehicle security and Motor Claims Framework awareness.

**FTT book:** safe-driver attitudes and accident prevention; controls, automatic transmission, switches, dashboard meters and warning indicators; seats, belts and mirrors; pre-drive checks; moving off/stopping, gears, bends, lane changes, junctions, roundabouts, overtaking, slopes, reversing, parking, directional changes, emergency stops and narrow courses; defensive driving; uphill/downhill driving; interactions with traffic, pedestrians, cyclists, motorcyclists, large vehicles and emergency vehicles; expressways; emergency response for vehicle faults; accident response; practical-test and learner-driver information; daily/weekly/periodic vehicle maintenance; light goods vehicles; traffic-light surveillance camera awareness; courtesy and driving-circuit context.

## Run locally
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open a terminal in this folder.
3. Run `python3 -m http.server 8000`
4. Open `http://localhost:8000`

## Install on a phone
Host all files on a static HTTPS website (for example GitHub Pages or Netlify). Open the HTTPS site on your phone. On iPhone use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android Chrome use Install app / Add to Home screen.

## Accuracy and copyright note
The cards and questions are concise, original paraphrases and summaries. They are not a page-by-page reproduction of either book, nor are the practice questions official test questions. Some rules have exceptions and operational details can change; verify exact legal wording, sign illustrations, penalties, dates and procedures in the current official handbook and Traffic Police/LTA sources. The app is not affiliated with the Singapore Police Force or CDC. Study history is stored locally and does not sync across devices.
