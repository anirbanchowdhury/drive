Coverage cross-check

This release adds study prompts derived from both uploaded Traffic Police handbooks: Basic Theory of Driving (90 pages, edition dated 2 Jan 2026) and Final Theory of Driving (118 pages, edition dated 2 Jan 2026). It now includes BTT sign/marking and traffic-rule topics plus FTT vehicle controls, driving techniques, defensive driving, emergency response, vehicle maintenance, work zones, tunnels, special zones, autonomous vehicles, eco-driving, courteous driving and accident/claims awareness. The study cards are paraphrased learning aids, not a verbatim copy of the books.

Important content note

Cards and questions are original summaries, not official Traffic Police questions. Some rules have exceptions; verify exact legal wording, signs, penalties and current rules in the official handbook. This app is independent and not affiliated with SPF or CDC.
