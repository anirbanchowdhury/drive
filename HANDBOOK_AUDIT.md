# BTT Ready Pro — Page-by-page handbook audit

Audit date: 22 October 2026  
Source files: `BT ENG 24126.pdf` (90 PDF pages) and `FT ENG 2126 Revised.pdf` (118 PDF pages), both published/updated 2 January 2026 by Singapore Traffic Police according to their title pages.

## Audit conclusion

**Result: NOT YET COMPLETE.** The current app is a useful study companion, but it cannot be certified as a full page-by-page representation of both handbooks. This audit maps all 208 PDF pages to their contents/section ranges and identifies gaps in the current study design. It does not claim that every diagram, sign illustration, table, exception, legal provision, or sentence has been individually converted into a question. Image-heavy pages require visual sign-by-sign verification, and the current question set is not a substitute for the official handbook.

## Main findings

### Basic Theory of Driving (90 PDF pages)
- Pages 9–49: sign, marking and signal illustrations require individual visual recognition cards. Broad text-only coverage is insufficient.
- Pages 3–8: licence eligibility, licence classes, PDL, new-driver restrictions, DIPS, medical examination and online portals need more exact coverage.
- Pages 51–70: road rules and code of conduct are covered at a high level, but exceptions, diagrams, numerical guidance and manoeuvre sequences need additional cards.
- Pages 71–79: labelled dashboard/control illustrations, work-zone signs, Friendly Streets, school/Silver Zones, tunnels, autonomous vehicles and electric motorcycles need more detailed checks.
- Pages 80–86: car-park signage/markings, lane-use signals and tunnel emergency facilities/procedures are not comprehensively represented.
- Pages 87–90: eco-driving, vehicle security and the Motor Claims Framework need explicit coverage. The app's generic accident advice is not enough to represent the MCF steps.

### Final Theory of Driving (118 PDF pages)
- Pages 5–23: vehicle controls, automatic-transmission selector positions, warning indicators, seating and mirror setup need diagram- and control-specific cards.
- Pages 24–74: moving off/stopping, changing gears, bends, lane changes, junctions, roundabouts, overtaking, slopes, reversing, vertical/parallel parking, directional change, emergency stops and narrow courses need step-by-step coverage.
- Pages 75–107: defensive driving, uphill/downhill driving, interactions with other road users, expressways and specific emergencies need scenario-specific cards. Generic hazard advice is not enough for the emergency chapters.
- Pages 108–118: test procedures, learner restrictions, maintenance schedules, LGV information, traffic-light surveillance cameras, courteous driving and the driving-circuit layout need separate coverage if the goal is both-handbook coverage.

## Page-by-page status key
- **Partially covered**: broad topic appears in the app, but page-specific details are not all represented.
- **Missing**: a meaningful topic in the page range is not represented sufficiently.
- **Needs verification**: the page could not be reliably mapped from extracted text alone and should be visually inspected.
- These statuses are conservative topic-level assessments, not a claim that each line on each page has been individually validated.

## Corrected app changes in this ZIP
- Adds a searchable 208-page audit report and CSV page ledger.
- Separates BTT-first preparation from FTT/practical driving material in the audit.
- Adds explicit warnings that the app is not an official question bank and that exact signs, exceptions and legal details must be checked against the handbook.
- Keeps original study questions as learning aids rather than reproducing the copyrighted books verbatim.

## Recommended next quality gate
For a true content-complete release, each illustrated sign/marking page and each procedure page must be visually inspected against the app's actual card/question bank. Then add source-page metadata to each relevant card and run a second reviewer pass for legal exceptions and ambiguous wording.
